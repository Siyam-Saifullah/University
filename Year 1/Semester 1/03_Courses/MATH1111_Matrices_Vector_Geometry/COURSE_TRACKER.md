# 📅 MATH1111 - Matrices, Vector Analysis and Co-ordinate Geometry
**Credits:** 3 | **Marks:** 75 | **Type:** Theoretical | **Contact Hours:** 3/week

## 📚 Course Content (from Curriculum)
| CLO | Topic | Subtopics | Study Materials | Status |
|-----|-------|-----------|-----------------|--------|
| CLO-1 | Algebra | Sets, De Morgan's rules, relations & functions; De Moivre's theorem; Relation between roots & coefficients; Cubic equations; Determinants: properties, Cramer's rule | `../../../../Study_Materials/Mathematics/Linear_Algebra/` | ☐ |
| CLO-2 | Co-ordinate Geometry (3D) | Cartesian, cylindrical, spherical polar coordinates; Transformation; Projections; Direction cosines/ratios; Angle between lines; Plane equations; Line equations; Spheres & tangent planes; Circles on spheres; Orthogonality of spheres | | ☐ |

| CLO-3 | Vector Analysis | Addition, multiplication, scalar/vector products, differentiation; Gradient, divergence, curl & physical significance; Line, surface, volume integrals; Green's, Stokes', Gauss theorems; Engineering applications | | ☐ |

## 📝 ILOs
1. Introduce fundamentals of algebra to solve mathematical equations
2. Familiarize understanding of trigonometric functions and calculating values
3. Present fundamental concepts of vector for science & engineering applications

## 🎯 CLOs
After completion, students should be able to:
1. Understand sets, relations, functions to solve systems of linear equations
2. Use geometric functions of complex argument for real-world problem solving
3. Perform basic matrix & vector operations (addition, subtraction, scalar multiplication) graphically & algebraically
