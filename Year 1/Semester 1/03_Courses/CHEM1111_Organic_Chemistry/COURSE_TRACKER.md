# 📅 CHEM1111 - Organic Chemistry
**Credits:** 2 | **Marks:** 50 | **Type:** Theoretical | **Contact Hours:** 2/week

## 📚 Course Content (from Curriculum)
| CLO | Topic | Subtopics | Status |
|-----|-------|-----------|--------|
| CLO-1 | General Concepts | Purification of organic compounds; Element detection; Bonding: σ/π bonds; Molecular shape; Delocalization & resonance | ☐ |
| CLO-2 | Aliphatic Compounds | Nomenclature; Preparation, properties, reactions & uses of aliphatic hydrocarbons; Mechanisms & homologous series: Alkyl halides, Grignard reagents, Alcohols, Aldehydes, Ethers, Ketones, Carboxylic acids & derivatives, Alkyl amines | ☐ |
| CLO-2 | Alicyclic Compounds | Nomenclature; Cyclopropane, cyclobutane, cyclohexane & derivatives; Ring formation & stability; Angle strain; Baeyer strain theory & weakness; Sachse-Mohr modifications | ☐ |
| CLO-2 | Aromatic Compounds | Benzene structure (Kekule & resonance); Aromaticity; Electrophilic & nucleophilic substitution mechanisms; Orientation & resonance; Substituent effects (activation/deactivation, orientation) | ☐ |
| CLO-3 | Biochemistry | Definition, characteristics, classification, structures & reactions of amino acids (isoelectric points); Proteins & carbohydrates | ☐ |
| CLO-3 | Stereochemistry | Stereoisomers; Asymmetric carbon & optical activity; Optical & molecular rotation; R/S configurations; Optical & geometrical isomerisms | ☐ |

## 📝 ILOs
1. Develop basic skills for multi-step synthesis & reaction mechanisms
2. Understand structure & reactivity of organic molecules with industrial examples
3. Apply chemical principles in laboratory setting

## 🎯 CLOs
After completion, students should be able to:
1. Gather & recall fundamental principles: purification, elemental analysis, bonding
2. Extend knowledge on nomenclature, preparation, properties, reactions & uses of aliphatic hydrocarbons
3. Understand biochemistry & stereochemistry basics
