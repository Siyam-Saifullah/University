1. Planning & implementation of advanced organic reactions
2. Basic chemistry techniques: percent yields, measuring devices
3. Detection of elements in organic compounds
4. Identification of functional groups
5. Determination & identification of metal ions

## 🎯 CLOs
After completion, students should be able to:
1. Identify metal & functional groups in organic compounds
2. Purify & characterize organic compounds
