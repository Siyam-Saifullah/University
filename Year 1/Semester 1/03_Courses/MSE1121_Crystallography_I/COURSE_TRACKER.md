# 📅 MSE1121 - Crystallography-I
**Credits:** 2 | **Marks:** 50 | **Type:** Theoretical | **Contact Hours:** 2/week
**Textbook:** Callister Ch. 3-4, plus references: Bloss, Hammond, Kittel, Mukherji

## 📚 Course Content (from Curriculum)
| CLO | Topic | Subtopics | Status |
|-----|-------|-----------|--------|
| CLO-1 | Crystalline Materials | Crystalline vs non-crystalline; Types: ionic, covalent, molecular, metallic; Cohesive energy; Lattice energy; Born-Haber cycle; Isomorphism, polymorphism, enantiotropy, monotropy | ☐ |
| CLO-1 | Crystals & Crystal Structures | Crystalline state; Faces/edges/interfacial angles; Space lattice; Unit cells; Periodicity; Atomic packing (hcp, ccp); Construction of crystals; Selected structures: metals, diamond/graphite, ionic (AB-type), silica, alumina, complex oxides, silicates; Crystallinity in polymers | ☐ |
| CLO-1 | Crystal Planes & Directions | Ion arrangements; Lattice planes; Miller indices & zone axes; Cubic crystal planes: spacing, interplanar distance, ratios; Miller vs Laue indices; Zones, zone laws; Addition rule; Trigonal/hexagonal indexing; Transforming indices | ☐ |
| CLO-2 | Crystal Imperfections | Effects on properties; Causes; Types: Point defects, Line defects, Plane defects, Volume defects | ☐ |

## 📝 ILOs
1. Describe different types of bonds in solid materials
2. Define lattice, crystal system, lattice plane and direction
3. Explain different types of defects in crystals

## 🎯 CLOs
After completion, students should be able to:
1. **Calculate** packing factor, density, interplanar spacing using geometrical parameters
2. **Clarify** which defect type has severe impact on material properties

## 📖 Recommended Books

2. C. Hammond - Introduction to Crystallography
3. C. Kittel - Introduction to Solid State Physics
4. Uma Mukherji - Engineering Physics
