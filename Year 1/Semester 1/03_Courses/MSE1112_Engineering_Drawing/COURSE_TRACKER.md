# 📅 MSE1132 - Engineering Drawing
**Credits:** 1 | **Marks:** 25 | **Type:** Sessional/Practical | **Contact Hours:** 3/week

## 📚 Course Description
Central issue: Help students visualize basics of engineering design and drawing. Improve hands-on experiences for practical life.
