# 📅 PHY1111 - Electricity and Magnetism
**Credits:** 3 | **Marks:** 75 | **Type:** Theoretical | **Contact Hours:** 3/week

## 📚 Course Content (from Curriculum)
| CLO | Topic | Subtopics | Status |
|-----|-------|-----------|--------|
| CLO-1 | Electrostatics | Electric field, potential difference & potential; Field/potential from charge distributions; Electric dipole; Field from dipole; Local atomic field; Dipole in external field; Gauss's Law & applications | ☐ |
| CLO-2 | Capacitance & Dielectrics | Parallel plate capacitors; Susceptibility, permeability, dielectric constant; Dielectric theory; Gauss's theorem in dielectrics; Capacitors with dielectrics; Energy in electric field | ☐ |
| CLO-3 | Thermoelectricity | Thermal emf; Seebeck, Peltier, Thomson effects; Laws of thermal emf addition; Thermoelectric power | ☐ |
| CLO-3 | Electric Current & Resistance | Current density; Resistance & Ohm's law; Resistivity; Electrical energy & power; Electron theory: conductors, semiconductors, insulators; Kirchhoff's laws; Superconductivity | ☐ |
| CLO-3 | Electromagnetic Induction | Faraday's experiment & law; Ampere's law; Motional emf; Self & mutual inductance; Galvanometers (moving coil, ballistic, deadbeat) | ☐ |
| CLO-3 | AC Theory & Frequency Domain | General AC theory, AC power, average & RMS values; Resonance phenomena; Q-value & bandwidth | ☐ |

## 📝 ILOs
1. Fundamental laws of physics based on electrostatics
2. Parallel plate capacitors using dielectrics & Gauss's law
3. Electric current based on electron theory
4. Electromagnetic induction using Faraday's & Ampere's laws
5. Principles of thermoelectricity; DC & AC circuits (series/parallel)
6. Techniques to analyze & solve physical problems with mathematics

## 🎯 CLOs
After completion, students should be able to:
1. Know theory of capacitance, applications using dielectrics & Gauss's law, electron theory of conductors
2. Use Coulomb's & Gauss's laws for electrostatic force & field-potential relationship
3. Use Lorentz force law for magnetic force, Ampere's law for magnetic fields
4. Use Faraday's law in induction problems & thermoelectricity laws
5. Know basic laws underlying electric circuit elements
