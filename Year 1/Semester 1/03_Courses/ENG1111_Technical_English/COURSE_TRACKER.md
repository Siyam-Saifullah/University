# 📅 ENG1111 - Technical and Communicative English
**Credits:** 2 | **Marks:** 50 | **Type:** Theoretical | **Contact Hours:** 2/week

## 📚 Course Content (from Curriculum)
| CLO | Topic | Subtopics | Status |
|-----|-------|-----------|--------|
| CLO-1 | Grammatical Principle | Sentences; Tenses; Clauses; Connectors; Long sentences; Conditionals; Transformation; Phrases (noun, adverbial, prepositional, verb); Modifiers; Head words; Appositives; Determiners; Suffixes/prefixes; Phrases & idioms; Modals; Gerunds; Infinitives; Bare infinitives; Causative verbs; Negation words | ☐ |
| CLO-1&2 | Spoken English | Greetings; Dialogues; Situational responses; Extempore speech | ☐ |
| CLO-1&2 | Reading | Comprehension: technical & non-technical; Skimming, scanning, inferring, contextual response | ☐ |
| CLO-1&2 | Technical Report & Presentation | Paragraph & composition writing (scientific/other); Report writing; Research paper writing; Library references | ☐ |
| CLO-1&2 | Professional Communication | Business letters; Job applications; Memos; Quotations; Tender notices | ☐ |

## 📝 ILOs
1. Develop & integrate four language skills: listening, speaking, reading, writing

## 🎯 CLOs
After completion, students should be able to:
1. Use English effectively for academic purposes across curriculum
2. Communicate effectively & appropriately in real-life situations

## 📖 Recommended Books
1. J. Thomson & A.V. Martinet - A Practical English Grammar
2. John M. Lennon - Technical Writing
3. A. Ashley - Oxford Handbook of Commercial Correspondence
4. J. Swales - Writing Scientific English
5. Robert J. Dixson - Complete Course in English
6. Rajendra Pal & J.S. Korlahalli - Essentials of Business Communications
