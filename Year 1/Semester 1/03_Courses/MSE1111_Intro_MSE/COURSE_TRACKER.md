# 📅 MSE1111 - Introduction to Materials Science and Engineering
**Credits:** 2 | **Marks:** 50 | **Type:** Theoretical | **Contact Hours:** 2/week
**Textbook:** Callister - Fundamentals of Materials Science and Engineering (5th Ed.), Ch. 1-3, 6-7

## 📚 Course Content (from Curriculum)
| CLO | Topic | Callister Ch. | Status |
|-----|-------|---------------|--------|
| CLO-1 | Understanding of Materials: Basic concepts, development, uses, advanced materials, modern needs, scope | 1 | ☐ |
| CLO-1 | Types of Solid Materials: Metals, polymers, ceramics, composites, semiconductors, crystalline/amorphous, superconductors | 3, 13, 14, 15 | ☐ |
| CLO-1&2 | Properties: Electrical/electronic, thermal/thermoelectric, mechanical, optical, magnetic | 6, 18, 19, 20 | ☐ |
| CLO-2&3 | Solidification: Nucleation/growth, homogeneous/heterogeneous, solid solutions, ordered/disordered, grain boundaries, cooling rate effects | 10 | ☐ |
| CLO-2&3 | Diffusion in Solids: Mechanisms, steady/non-steady state, porous media, Fick's laws, transient, factors, other paths | 5 | ☐ |
| CLO-3 | Materials Selection & Design: Importance, factors, relation to design, product analysis, development activities | 22 | ☐ |

## 📝 ILOs (Intended Learning Objectives)
1. Familiarize students about importance of materials science in modern civilization
2. Classify metals, ceramics, polymers, electronic materials via atomic structure & interatomic bonding
3. Build ability to relate processing → structure → physical properties

## 🎯 CLOs (Course Learning Outcomes)
After completion, students should be able to:
1. **Classify** materials
2. **Understand** basic properties characterizing material behavior
3. **Relate** processing, structure, and physical properties
